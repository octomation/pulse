# v0.2.1 — Change your mind.

Decide without leaving the flow, see on every card what you decided before, and take any answer back in one move. `pulsectl bookmarks` now hands over only the repositories you marked as interesting that have no note in your vault yet.

## Flow mode shows the whole story

- A flow card is the repository as the reader shows it: the stars, the forks, the open issues, the license, its age and last push, its topics, its links and the README, without a click. The reader no longer opens over the flow.
- The answers stay in the bar and work at any time, also after a click into the README: `←` skips, `→` marks as interesting, `↓` leaves the card for later.
- `↑` goes back to the previous card, with the answer it got and its comment; another answer replaces it, the same one writes nothing.
- `u` takes back the answer of the card on the screen, and `c` opens the comment for **Interesting**. [Flow mode →](/trend-watch/#flow-mode)

## Every card says where it stands

- A stamp shows the answer: `★ Interesting`, `Skipped · until Feb 23, 2027`, `Seen · until …` after **Mark all shown as seen**, or `In your notes` when a note of your vault names the repository.
- **Show hidden** groups the hidden repositories under **Interesting**, **Skipped** and **In your notes**.
- A repository in your notes is not offered **Interesting** or **Skip** again. [Hidden repositories →](/trend-watch/#hidden-repositories)

## Any answer can be taken back

- Your last answer counts: **Skip** after **Interesting** removes the bookmark, **Interesting** after **Skip** ends the snooze.
- **Take back** in the reader makes a repository new again; a note in your vault stays as it is.
- A second **Skip** no longer moves the end of the snooze.
- **Mark all shown as seen** offers **Undo**, which takes back exactly the marks it wrote. [The page →](/trend-watch/#the-page)

## The interest without a note

- `pulsectl bookmarks` prints the repositories marked as interesting that no note of your vault names yet: the list to turn into notes. `pulsectl bookmarks --all` prints every bookmark, as before.
- When the vault or one of its notes cannot be read, `pulsectl bookmarks` fails and names the cause instead of printing what may have a note already.
- **Queue**, at `/queue/`, shows the same list, also the repositories that left the lists, with **Take back** on each. [The queue →](/trend-watch/#the-queue)

## Pictures of a README

- A README picture that has a light and a dark version, such as the banner of nginx, now shows in the reader and in flow mode.
- A picture that GitHub shows from its own address when its image proxy cannot fetch it now shows too.

**Upgrade:** on macOS, run `brew update` followed by `brew upgrade --formula octolab/tap/pulse`, then `brew services restart octolab/tap/pulse`. With Go, run `go install go.octolab.org/toolset/pulse/cmd/...@v0.2.1`. For a manual installation, use a [v0.2.1 release archive](https://github.com/octomation/pulse/releases/tag/v0.2.1). Run `pulse version` and `pulsectl version` to check the installed version. A script that reads `pulsectl bookmarks` now gets only the bookmarks without a note: add `--all` to keep every one. Once you take an answer back, pulse v0.2.0 cannot open its data directory again.
