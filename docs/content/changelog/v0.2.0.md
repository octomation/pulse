# v0.2.0 — Catch up.

pulse now remembers every morning. Open a day you skipped from the calendar, go through the new repositories one card at a time in flow mode, and see a repository again when it trends after you skipped it three months ago.

## A calendar of past days

- Click the date in the masthead: the days that have loads of the lists are links, and the arrows step between them.
- `http://pulse.localhost:17842/days/2026-11-19/` shows the lists as they stood that day. What is hidden is decided by what you have done since: a repository you skipped today is hidden on yesterday's page too.
- **Interesting**, **Skip** and **Mark all shown as seen** work on a past day: in the reader, in flow mode and in the masthead. A past day loads nothing from the network, and its sections are **That day**, **That week** and **That month**.
- **Show hidden** is now an address of its own: `/hidden/` for today, `/days/YYYY-MM-DD/hidden/` for a day. The old `/?hidden=1` opens today without the hidden ones. [Past days →](/trend-watch/#past-days)

## Flow mode

- **Flow** shows the new repositories one card at a time, with **Skip**, **Later** and **Interesting** always in the same place at the bottom of the screen.
- `←` skips, `→` bookmarks with the comment you typed, `↓` leaves the card for later; on a touch screen, swipe left or right.
- The flow of today is at `/flow/`, the flow of a past day at `/days/YYYY-MM-DD/flow/`. [Flow mode →](/trend-watch/#flow-mode)

## A page that reads as a newspaper

- The cards stand in one grid of three columns from the front page to the last section, two on a narrow screen and one on a phone; the language, name, description and lists of a row are on one line.
- The lead story spans two columns, and the next two stand beside it.
- A card is for reading: click its name or its description to open the reader, which has the comment field, **Interesting** and **Skip**, or go through the cards in flow mode. **Mark all shown as seen** stays in the masthead.
- A card shows up to three lines of its description, the lead five; the reader shows it whole. [The page →](/trend-watch/#the-page)

## A skip lasts three months

- **Skip** and **Mark all shown as seen** hide a repository for three calendar months. When it trends again after that, it is back on the page; under **Show hidden** a card says `Skipped on Nov 20, 2026, until Feb 20, 2027`.
- The marks you already have follow the new rule: a repository you skipped more than three months ago is shown again when a list has it.
- **Interesting** still hides for good, and so does a note in your vault.
- To choose another period, set it in `~/.config/pulse/config.toml`: [Choose how long a skip lasts →](/trend-watch/#choose-how-long-a-skip-lasts)

```toml
[seen]
snooze = "1 month"
```

## The vault you read, at a glance

- The vault line names the directory pulse reads, the number of repositories its notes name and the time of the newest note, as in `Vault: 2712 repositories in 2647 notes · ~/Notes/Bookmarks/GitHub · newest note Nov 22, 2026, 20:45.`
- When that directory is not inside a vault Obsidian knows on this computer, the page warns that it may be an old copy.
- When the vault cannot be read, the page never says **Nothing new** without saying that the list may include what is already there. [Point it at your bookmarks →](/trend-watch/#point-it-at-your-bookmarks)

**Upgrade:** on macOS, run `brew update` followed by `brew upgrade --formula octolab/tap/pulse`, then `brew services restart octolab/tap/pulse`. With Go, run `go install go.octolab.org/toolset/pulse/cmd/...@v0.2.0`. For a manual installation, use a [v0.2.0 release archive](https://github.com/octomation/pulse/releases/tag/v0.2.0). Run `pulse version` and `pulsectl version` to check the installed version. A bookmark of `/?hidden=1` in your browser now opens today without the hidden ones: change it to `/hidden/`.
